What Record Preview does

Record Preview helps you find music previews while browsing record listings on supported websites. It reads listing information in your browser, searches for possible music matches, and can show a YouTube video in an embedded player when you choose to play it. Search results may be incomplete or incorrect, and a suitable preview may not be available.
