A small tool for record browsing

Hear the record while you shop.

Record Preview is a Chrome extension that finds YouTube music previews for physical record listings, so you can listen without leaving the shop page.

Privacy policy Contact

How it works

01 · Browse

Open a supported record shop and move over a CD, vinyl, or album listing.

02 · Identify

The extension reads the listing’s artist and release title in your browser. It uses track information when the shop provides it.

03 · Find a match

Record Preview searches YouTube for possible music matches and filters out common unrelated results. It shows the candidate title and match confidence.

04 · Preview

Choose a result and play it in the compact preview card. Playback is controlled by you and stops when the temporary card closes.

Supported shops

Record Preview currently supports Discogs, Yahoo! Auctions Japan, Alley Tunes, and Dutch Vinyl. Listing support can vary with each shop’s page layout and the information it provides.

Search and playback

YouTube search uses the YouTube Data API and the quota assigned to Record Preview’s project. The quota is shared by searches made through the distributed extension, so previews may be unavailable when the project reaches its daily limit. YouTube search results and embedded playback are supplied by Google and remain subject to Google’s terms and availability.

Record Preview does not guarantee that every record has a suitable YouTube preview. If it cannot find a confident candidate, it shows that no preview is available rather than silently presenting an unrelated album.

Project details

Record Preview is an independent Chrome extension project by Jake Pearce. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discogs, Yahoo! Auctions, Alley Tunes, Dutch Vinyl, or YouTube.
